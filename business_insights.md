# Retail Sales Analysis – Key Business Insights

## 1. Category Performance
- Electronics generated the highest revenue of ₹3,13,810, contributing approximately 34.4% of total revenue.
- Clothing recorded the highest transaction count (701) and quantity sold (1,785 units).
- Beauty achieved the highest average selling price of ₹184.57.

## 2. Monthly Sales Trends
- December 2022 recorded the highest monthly revenue at ₹72,880.
- February 2022 recorded the lowest monthly revenue at ₹16,110.
- Revenue increased from ₹4,52,825 in 2022 to ₹4,58,895 in 2023, representing approximately 1.34% growth.
- September to December showed a recurring increase in sales activity across both years.

## 3. Customer Spending Patterns
- Customer 3 was the highest-spending customer, generating ₹38,440 across 76 transactions.
- The top five customers collectively generated ₹1,48,470, approximately 16.28% of total revenue.

## 4. Customer Retention
- All 155 customers in the dataset made more than one transaction.
- No one-time customers were identified in the dataset.
- This indicates repeat-purchase activity among the recorded customers, but does not establish the business's overall retention rate.

## 5. Category Profitability
- Clothing generated the highest gross profit at ₹2,46,679.50.
- Beauty recorded the highest gross profit margin at 79.71%.
- Electronics generated the highest revenue but had the lowest gross profit margin at 78.60%.

## 6. Gender-wise Sales Performance
- Male customers generated ₹4,56,030 in revenue, slightly higher than female customers at ₹4,55,690.
- Female customers recorded more transactions and purchased more units.
- Male customers had a higher average sale value of ₹465.81 compared with ₹447.63 for female customers.

---

*Note: All findings are based on the available retail sales dataset covering 2022–2023.*
